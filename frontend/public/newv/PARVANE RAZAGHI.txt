PROFILE
Parvaneh Razaghi is a professional Iranian painter whose artistic practice encompasses figurative and contemporary painting. Her work explores the human figure, emotion, imagination, and psychological narratives through a personal visual language.
She has participated in numerous solo and group exhibitions in Iran and internationally, with exhibitions and art events in countries including Switzerland, Italy, Russia, Portugal, France, and China.
